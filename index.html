Pain plan at home

  1. Start your prescribed pain medicine early, at the first sign of a crisis.
  2. Drink extra water and keep warm. Avoid ice packs.
  3. Rest, use a heating pad, and log your pain every 2–3 hours.
  4. If pain is not better after your planned doses, call the clinic or go to the ER.
